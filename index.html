Dr. Roshan Paudel is a professor of Practice and graduate program director in the Bioinformatics department at Morgan State University in Baltimore, Maryland. His research includes computational thinking, cardiac modeling, RNA/protein structure prediction, gene expression analysis, and AI-driven detection of genetic biomarkers. He has participated in research spanning bioinformatics, AI, 3D structure prediction, genomics, and metagenomics, with numerous peer-reviewed journal and conference publications in these areas. Dr. Paudel earned his Ph.D. in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology from George Mason University in Fairfax, VA, and his master’s in Bioinformatics from Morgan State University. He also holds undergraduate and graduate degrees from Tribhuvan University in Kathmandu, Nepal. His teaching duties include undergraduate courses in computer science and graduate-level Bioinformatics courses, including Introduction to Computer Science I & II, Structural Bioinformatics & Drug Design, Computational Genomics, and Bioinformatics Programming. He has conducted workshops on R programming, Machine Learning with R & Python, and Ingenuity Pathway Analysis (IPA) for faculty, students, and postdocs. Additionally, he collaborated with Google and faculty from various US universities to develop a GenAI-enhanced curriculum and teaching materials for introductory computer science courses, which are now adopted by 15 HBCUs for their freshmen through CodePath. He aims to utilize Data Science courses to analyze healthcare data, supporting disease diagnosis and prognosis
